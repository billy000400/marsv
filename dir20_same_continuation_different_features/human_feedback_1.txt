The fixed-path intervention problem is now resolved, and the matched association is substantially cleaner. However, this cannot be described as a fully pre-registered confirmatory result: the PLAN fixed 300 prefixes and explicitly required an underpowered verdict and stop when fewer than 40 contrasts survived, but the bank was expanded from 300 to 1,395 prefixes after only 21 survived. Because no widths had been observed, this blinded sample-size change does not necessarily bias the effect, but it violates the frozen stopping rule. Please relabel this as an amended analysis and require an independent replication before making a confirmatory claim.
